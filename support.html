Frequently Asked Questions

What is Marble Parlour?

Marble Parlour is a collection of seven traditional marble-board games — Star Checkers, Marble Solitaire, Track Race, Mancala, Nine Men's Morris, Marble Drop, and Code Break — playable solo against the computer, pass-and-play, with a nearby device, or online through Game Center.

Does the app require an internet connection?

No. Solo games against the computer, pass-and-play, and the Daily Challenge all work fully offline. Nearby play connects two devices directly over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth. Only online matches through Game Center need an internet connection.

I bought the Parlour Pass but it's not showing up.

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases, or tap Restore purchase on the Parlour Pass screen. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase. Non-consumable purchases also restore automatically when you reinstall the app on a device signed in to that Apple ID.

Is my data collected?

No. We do not collect any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no ads. Please see our Privacy Policy for details.

The app is crashing or not loading correctly.

Please try the following steps:

  • Make sure your device is running iOS 17 or later.
  • Close the app completely and reopen it.
  • Restart your device.
  • If the issue persists, delete and reinstall the app (any non-consumable purchases can be restored for free).

If none of these help, please email us and include your device model and OS version.
